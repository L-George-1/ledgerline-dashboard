Ledgerline Demand Generation and Cost of Acquisition Reporting

This dashboard is built from 8 CSV exports from the Ledgerline data room. Export the files listed below, then select all 8 at once with the button. You can hold Cmd (Mac) or Ctrl (Windows) to pick several files, or press Cmd+A / Ctrl+A in a folder that holds only these files.

The files stay on this computer: nothing is uploaded anywhere, and nothing is saved when you close the tab. File names don't have to match. The app recognizes each file by its column headings, so a renamed file works as long as it has the columns shown.

            Definitions and data notes
            • Lead: every record in leads.csv, dated by the month it was created. MQL: a lead minus junk (spam/test, existing customers, repeat submissions), minus Disqualified leads whose reason is student or researcher, vendor pitch, too small or suspected competitor. Disqualified leads with no reason stay MQLs, as do Nurture or Working leads tagged too small.
            • SAL: status Working or Converted to opportunity with no disqualification reason. Working leads that carry a reason (mostly existing customers) are not counted. SALs are dated by lead creation month; leads.csv has no acceptance date.
            • Opp: a new-business opportunity linked from a lead, dated by created date. Won: Closed won, dated by close date. 16 opps created before Jan 2023 have no source lead; their 14 customers appear on the Churn tab and in the explorer, not in these counts.
            • Demand gen cost, inbound: program spend + the slider's share of Marketing payroll + the SDR's loaded cost split by MQL share. Payroll marked Other (not demand gen) is excluded from demand gen cost and Full CAC. Outbound: 20 AE hours/week × loaded AE rate (—/hr in —).
            • Sales-side cost: Sales payroll minus SDR and outbound prospecting cost. Full CAC = demand gen + sales side, per customer won. CAC ratio = that total ÷ new ARR won (excludes commissions).
            • Thin data: cost per lead/MQL/SAL/opp flagged under 10 records in the stage; cost per customer and ACV under 5 wins; conversion rates under 30 records in the denominator.
            • Period-based: every count lands in the month it happened, so conversion rates mix cohorts.

            Definitions
            • Source: the customer's acquisition channel, which matches the source channel of its won opportunity in every case. Plan: Core, Growth or Enterprise; no customer changed plan.
            • Measurable at 12 / 24 mo: signed at least 12 / 24 months before —. 12-mo churn: of those, the share whose churn date falls within 12 months of signup. Contracts are annual, so this is the first-renewal loss rate.
            • ARR lost: the last contract ARR of customers who churned. Thin data: churn rates flagged under 20 measurable customers.
